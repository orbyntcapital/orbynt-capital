What we are

A private fund, several strategies.

Orbynt Capital is a private investment fund. We invest across financial markets, with an increased concentration in digital assets like crypto and Web3.

Rather than rely on a single strategy, we run a family of funds. Each one has its own objective, approach, and risk profile, so capital can be matched to the outcome it is meant to achieve. Three funds are live and managing capital today; two more are in development.

01

Multiple mandates

We run several funds at once, each built for a different objective and level of risk. That breadth lets us perform across very different market conditions.

02

Specialist focus

Our attention is concentrated on digital asset markets, where dedicated expertise and infrastructure still produce a genuine edge.

03

Risk before return

Every position sits inside a defined risk framework. Limits and sizing are set before any capital is committed.

04

Proprietary methods

Our models, strategies, and infrastructure are built and operated in-house, and how they work stays private.
