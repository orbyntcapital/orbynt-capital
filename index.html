Our standards.

The following standards apply to every fund we manage.

Confidentiality

Information concerning our partners, holdings and research is held in strict confidence. It is disclosed only to parties with a direct relationship with the fund, and only to the extent required.

Risk management

Each fund operates within defined limits on exposure, concentration and drawdown. These limits are established before capital is deployed and are monitored on a continuous basis.

Governance

Every fund is managed under a defined mandate that sets out its objective, risk tolerance and scope of investment. Changes to a mandate are reviewed and recorded before they take effect.

Long-term orientation

Our funds are managed with a multi-year horizon. Decisions are assessed against long-term outcomes rather than short-term performance.
